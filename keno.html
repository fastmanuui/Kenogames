Keno is a game where the player bets on balls numbered 1-80 by choosing a combination from balls numbered 1 to 10.

During each round, 20 of the balls numbered from 1-80 are drawn in sequence using a random number generator.

How to play

To participate in the game, the player must perform the following actions during the round, which lasts one minute:

  • Choose the combination of numbers
  • Set the amount limit for betting
  • Click the “Bet” button

The player can also delete the combination of already selected numbers.

HOT and COLD numbers are shown with a small coloured dot (orange = hot, blue = cold).

Game Policy

Fast Keno is a skill-and-chance based multiplayer game. All draws are generated by a certified Random Number Generator (RNG).

• Players must be 18 years or older.
• Bets are final once placed and cannot be cancelled after the betting window closes.
• Winnings are credited automatically after each draw.
• We reserve the right to void tickets in case of technical issues or abuse.
• Responsible gaming is encouraged. Play within your means.

By placing a bet you agree to these terms.

Random Number Generator

Our RNG is independently tested and certified. Every draw is completely random and cannot be predicted or influenced by players or the house.

The system uses cryptographically secure entropy sources to guarantee fairness and integrity on every single round.
